Undergraduate Researcher · Computer Science

Yalan He

Exploring intelligent interactive systems that understand people and support human capabilities.

I am a final-year undergraduate studying Computer Science and Technology at Fudan University, and a former exchange student at Aalto University. My interests lie at the intersection of human-computer interaction, human-AI interaction, and computational methods for understanding human behavior.

My research experiences span physical human-computer interaction, human motion analysis, human-AI collaboration, and graph-based modeling. I am particularly interested in how assistive systems can adapt to people while supporting their learning, judgment, and control.
